Sorry! BAZINGA! didn't start

Something got in the way while the app was loading. Your music and settings are safe on this device.

Things that usually fix it:

  1. Check your internet connection, then tap Try again.
  2. Close the BAZINGA! tab or app completely and open it again.
  3. Turn off ad or content blockers for this site (Brave Shields, Safari content blockers, private DNS).
  4. Leave Private / Incognito mode — it can block the storage BAZINGA! needs.
  5. Update your browser. iPhone and iPad need iOS 15 or newer; or try Chrome, Edge or Safari.
  6. Still stuck? Tap Repair app. It reloads BAZINGA!'s files without touching your music.
