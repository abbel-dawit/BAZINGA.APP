Privacy Policy

What we collect

If you use BAZINGA! without an account: nothing. There are no analytics and no advertising, and we never receive your username, profile picture, music library, listening history or backups.

If you create an account (optional), we store, in our Supabase database and storage: your email address (or the name and email your Google or Discord account shares with us), your username and profile picture, the date you accepted these terms, and a copy of your music library (songs, likes, playlists, albums, YouTube matches and app settings, but never your API keys, tokens or offline music files) so it can sync between your devices. Passwords are handled by Supabase and stored only as secure hashes. We don't sell or share this data with anyone, and only you can read it. You can delete your account and all of this data at any time in Settings → Delete account; signing out lets you keep or remove the copy on a device.

What is stored on your device

Your username, profile picture, library, playlists, albums, YouTube matches, settings, any music files you add, any Spotify sign-in token, and automatic backups are stored only in your browser's storage on your device (and in any backup file you choose to save). You can erase all of it at any time with Settings → Delete account.

Third-party services your device talks to

To work, the App sends requests directly from your device to these services, which receive your IP address and the search terms or IDs needed. Their own privacy policies apply:

  • YouTube / Google — to show and play videos with the official YouTube player, and, if you add your own API key, to search YouTube.
  • Public Invidious and Piped servers (independent, community-run) — to search for matching YouTube videos without an API key.
  • Apple iTunes Search API — to look up genres, release dates, artwork, albums, artists and new releases.
  • Deezer API — to find missing cover art and artist photos.
  • Genius — only when you open lyrics: to find the song and show Genius's official lyrics widget (and, if you add one, using your own Genius access token).
  • Spotify — only if you choose to connect it, to read your Liked Songs and playlists.
  • Supabase — only if you create an account: hosts accounts, sign-in emails and your synced library.
  • Google or Discord sign-in — only if you choose "Continue with Google/Discord": shares your name, email and picture with BAZINGA! for your account.
  • Google / YouTube account — only if you choose to connect it (see below).
  • Buy Me a Coffee — only if you choose to send a tip; payments happen on their site and BAZINGA! never sees your payment details.
  • Google Fonts and GitHub Pages — to load the App's fonts and files.

YouTube account connection

If you connect your YouTube account, BAZINGA! uses Google's sign-in to ask for permission to manage your YouTube playlists. It uses that permission only to (a) list your playlists and their videos when you choose to import them, and (b) create playlists and add videos to them when you choose "Save to YouTube". It never deletes anything, never uploads videos, never posts comments, and never reads anything else from your account. The access token Google issues is stored only on your device, expires after about an hour, and is never sent to us — we have no server. You can disconnect in Settings at any time, and revoke access completely at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

BAZINGA! uses YouTube API Services. By using these features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Voice assistant

The voice assistant only listens after you tap the microphone, and stops after one request. Speech recognition is provided by your browser or operating system (for example Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari, Microsoft in Edge), which may process your voice on their servers under their own privacy policies. BAZINGA! itself never records, stores or sends your voice anywhere. If you prefer, you can type requests instead.

Sharing

When you share a song, playlist or album, its song list (and your username) is packed into the link itself. Anyone you give the link to can read it. Nothing is uploaded by the App.
